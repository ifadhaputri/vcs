On board

Dinner, then a bed.

The dining car opens as the train pulls away from the city. Tables are laid with linen and small brass lamps, giving you a quiet place to eat while town lights turn to dark countryside outside your window.

Morning service

Breakfast is already arranged.

Before the train rolls into the terminal, morning service opens in both the dining car and in your cabin. Fresh filter coffee, loose leaf tea, warm baker basket sourdough, croissants, butter, and fruit preserves are provided for every guest.

Included with every berth reservation. No extra booking required.

Dining car schedule

Table hours.

The car remains open throughout the evening and early morning. The kitchen closes thirty minutes before train arrival to allow all carriages to prepare for terminal stops.

20:30 – 21:30
Welcome drinks and small plates

Served immediately after boarding. Warm broth, local beer, natural wine, and light bites while carriages settle into the night rhythm.

21:00 – 22:15
Dinner service, first seating

Full three-course dining car menu. Reserved for guests wishing to finish dinner before late departure stops.

22:30 – 23:45
Dinner service, second seating

Unrushed late supper as the train hits cruising speed on the open mainlines. Drinks service continues until midnight.

06:30 – 08:30
Morning breakfast service

Warm bread, fresh coffee, and breakfast trays. The dining car closes 30 minutes prior to final station arrival.

Dietary requirements

Vegetarian, vegan, and gluten-free choices

Every service includes thoughtful vegetarian and plant-based courses, alongside gluten-free bread and alternatives. Please let the crew know your preferences when reserving your ticket or notify the car steward at boarding so the galley can prepare your meal properly.
